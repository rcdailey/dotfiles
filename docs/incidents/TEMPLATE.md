# Symptom in a few words (component and version)

Copy to `YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md`, dated by the first occurrence. Replace every placeholder and delete
sections marked optional when empty. Update Status, Current state, and Next check in place on each
follow-up; record each change made to the system as a new dated entry under Actions.

- Date: YYYY-MM-DD of first occurrence
- Status: Open | Mitigated | Resolved: short detail (YYYY-MM-DD of last update)
- System: OS, hardware, and the versions involved

## Symptom

What happens, when, and how to trigger it.

## Investigation

Optional. Logs, what was ruled out, and upstream reports, in subsections as needed.

## Cause

Start with "Confirmed." or "Suspected, not confirmed.", then the cause and its evidence. Correct
earlier conclusions here instead of leaving them standing elsewhere.

## Actions

One dated entry per change made to the system, with the exact commands and how to revert it.

## Current state

What is in place now and what remains unverified, as of a date.

## Next check

Numbered steps to recheck or verify the fix. Add a "Risk while ..." subsection when the current
workaround can break.
